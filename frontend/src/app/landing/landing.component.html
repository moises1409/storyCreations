How It Works

Create your characters

Design unique characters for your story by uploading photos, taking pictures, or describing them. Bring your heroes to life!

Describe how the story should start

Tell us how the story should begin. We'll create the first chapter with your characters.

Continue the adventure

Decide what happens next and build the tale together, chapter by chapter. You can edit the generated chapter and add more characters to the story.

Finish & treasure

Save it to your library, generate audio narration, and share your story with family and friends on social networks and WhatsApp. You can also turn it into a printed book you’ll treasure.
